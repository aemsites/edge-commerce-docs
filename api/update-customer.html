Customers

Create or update your profile

PATCH /{org}/sites/{site}/customers/{email}

Base URL https://api.adobecommerce.live

Staging https://api-stage.adobecommerce.live

Creates the caller's customer record if missing, or partially updates it if present. Provided first-class fields overwrite, the custom bag is shallow-merged, and unlisted fields are preserved. Email is taken from the path; a body email is rejected. Requires ownership of the email and membership of the org/site (no customers:write permission).
